# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Research faculty, PhD students, research labs, and teams hiring for AI/ML research or research engineering. They are evaluating Haider's research direction, technical depth, and ability to build working systems.

## Product Purpose

A one-page research profile for Haider Dawood Khan that presents his current identity as an M.S. Computer Science and Engineering student at the University of Michigan and an emerging machine-learning researcher. Success means a visitor can quickly understand his research interests, inspect selected work and results, follow his experience, and contact him.

## Brand Personality

Precise, curious, understated. The site should feel intellectually confident and research-focused, with enough warmth to remain personal.

## Anti-references

- Generic undergraduate software-engineering portfolios and SaaS landing-page templates.
- The current site's split home/projects views and outdated student status.
- Neon cyberpunk, gratuitous AI imagery, excessive gradients or glass effects, and badge-heavy layouts.
- The reference portfolio's specific palette or a direct copy of its composition.

The reference portfolio at https://ha405.github.io/portfolio/#work is useful for its scrolling project storytelling, typography, and restrained composition.

## Design Principles

- Lead with Haider's current Michigan and machine-learning research identity.
- Show depth through research questions, methods, and measured results.
- Make the page read as a coherent story from interests to experience to selected work.
- Keep claims concise, technically specific, and grounded in the CV or verifiable project material.
- Present engineering breadth as support for a research profile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic structure, keyboard-accessible navigation and visible focus states, readable contrast targeting WCAG AA, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion alternatives.
