About Me

I am Haider Dawood Khan, a senior Computer Science student at the Lahore University of Management Sciences (LUMS). My research interests lie in Applied Deep Learning, Machine Learning, and Large Language Models, with a broader curiosity for anything ML-related.

I currently serve as the Senior Lab Lead at the Center for Speech and Language Technologies (CSaLT), working under the mentorship of Dr. Agha Ali Raza. At the lab, I am currently reseaching on the generalizability and interpretablity of audio deepfake detection models and the accuracy-fairness tradeoff in LLMs.

Outside academics, I am an avid cricket fan - watching, playing, and obsessing over the sport whenever I can. I love staying active and enjoy a range of sports including badminton, padel, and table tennis. I also love to travel, explore new places, and experience different cultures.

My (Selected) Projects

A selection of my academic research and technical work.

Inductive Biases & Generalization in Deep Learning Models

Inductive Biases, OOD Generalization, Vision Models

A comparative study of CNNs, ViTs, VAEs, GANs, and CLIP to uncover how architectural and semantic inductive biases shape robustness and out-of-distribution performance. Evaluated color/texture/shape bias, invariance properties, generative behavior, and zero-shot transfer under domain shifts.

Domain Adaptation, Generalization & Prompt Tuning Across Vision and Vision-Language Models

Domain Robustness, CLIP Tuning, Cross-Domain Learning

A comprehensive evaluation of domain adaptation (DAN, DANN, CDAN), domain generalization (ERM, IRM, SAM, GDRO), and prompt-tuning strategies across PACS domains. Shows how invariance, discriminability, and prompt drift shape performance in unseen or stylistically shifted domains.

View Report

Federated Learning Under Heterogeneity: Drift, Convergence & Algorithmic Trade-offs

Statistical Heterogeneity, Client Drift, Non-IID

A deep empirical study comparing FedSGD, FedAvg, FedProx, FedSAM, FedGH, and SCAFFOLD under varying data heterogeneity. Highlights how drift correction, sharpness-aware updates, and gradient harmonization affect convergence and accuracy on non-IID clients.

View Report

Model Compression

Pruning, Quantization & Knowledge Distillation

An evaluation of pruning, quantization, and distillation techniques on VGG and ResNet models to assess accuracy–efficiency trade-offs. Shows how structured pruning, mixed-precision quantization, and feature-based distillation yield the best balance for edge deployment.
