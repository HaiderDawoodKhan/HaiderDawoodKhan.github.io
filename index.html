02 Efficient ML

Model compression for efficient inference

On VGG-11, 70% unstructured sparsity raised accuracy by 1.24%. Channel pruning cut MACs by 68.7% and latency by 65%, retaining 91.84% accuracy. For ResNet, mixed precision matched FP32 at ~4× smaller size and QAT beat PTQ; Hints distillation reached 67.73% on CIFAR-100.

Generative image modeling & diffusion

Implemented a class-conditioned U-Net DDPM with a cosine noise schedule and sinusoidal time embeddings, and explored β-VAE representations on CelebA and autoregressive PixelCNN generation. Stable Diffusion experiments covered classifier-free guidance, SD 2 inpainting, and style transfer with Canny ControlNet and IP-Adapter.

04 Computer vision

Automated number plate detection & logging

Fine-tuned a YOLO detector for plate localization, then combined contour-based character segmentation with a CNN classifier. A Streamlit search and logging interface retrieves matching vehicle images and metadata from plate-text queries.

05 Edge ML

Edge AI human activity recognition

Trained a six-class activity classifier on 20 Hz IMU windows and applied mixed-precision post-training quantization. Deployed it to an ESP32 with an MPU6050 sensor, serving live predictions through a lightweight Flask interface.

06 RLHF · GRPO

Token-level clipping analysis

Instrumented GRPO to track token-level clipping and build a vocabulary around refusal and moral-violation cues while aligning Qwen3-0.6B and OLMo-1B-SFT on Do-Not-Answer. Compared vocabulary-freeze and no-clipping interventions, then measured refusal rates with Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct as an LLM judge. The analysis examined how high-entropy safety tokens and clipping affect training stability.
